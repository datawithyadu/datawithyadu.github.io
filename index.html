Agentic AI

Smart Task Planner

ReAct agent · Notion-backed notes

A ReAct agent that reads, writes and retires notes in a live Notion workspace. Built on LangGraph with Groq's llama-3.1-8b-instant, with three tools the model can reach for: get_notes, add_notes, and a search-then-trash tool.

  • Shipped through Notion's 2025-09-03 API migration, moving the integration from the databases endpoints to data sources.
  • Postmortem finding: debugging through the full agent loop resent the tool schema and the whole conversation on every cycle. Testing tools directly against .invoke() cut the cost of the same debugging work to near zero — without changing the architecture.

Research

Multimodal Automotive Defect Analysis

MSc thesis · Complaint text + damage images

Master's thesis on classifying automotive defects from two signals that are usually handled separately: the free-text complaint a customer writes, and the photograph of the damage itself. The work compares text-only, image-only and fused models on the same task.

  • Fifty-nine pages, thirteen tables, forty-five citations; architecture diagrams rebuilt from scratch in TikZ.
  • Condensed into a journal-format paper prepared to Elsevier's Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence requirements.
  • Bibliography audited source by source, with misattributed citations traced and replaced.

Cloud architecture

Student Records Platform

AWS Academy capstone · Four phases

A web application for student records, architected end to end on AWS across four delivery phases — from a single-instance prototype to a load-balanced, auto-scaling deployment with secrets kept out of the codebase.

  • VPC networking, RDS for persistence, EC2 behind an Application Load Balancer with an Auto Scaling group.
  • CloudWatch for monitoring and alarms; Secrets Manager for database credentials.
  • Completed with the optional bonus components on top of the required scope.

Deep learning · Team

Audio-Visual Event Localization

AVE dataset · BiLSTM with audio-guided attention

Team project on localizing events in video by using sound and vision together: an audio-guided attention mechanism weights the visual features, and a bidirectional LSTM reads the resulting sequence to decide what is happening, and when.

  • Trained and evaluated on the AVE benchmark dataset.
  • My contribution ran to debugging the training pipeline and validating results across runs.

About

I came to AI from the business side of the table

For five years my job was to sit between people who needed something and the systems that were supposed to deliver it — writing the requirements, wiring up the KPIs, keeping ERP rollouts honest across logistics operations.

That work taught me the part of AI projects that models don't cover: a system is only useful if someone can explain what it does, what it costs to run, and what happens when it is wrong. So when I moved into data science, I kept the habit. I finished an MSc in Data Science in Berlin with a thesis on multimodal defect classification, and I have been building agentic systems since — each one with a written postmortem covering architecture decisions, token economics, and the trade-offs I would revisit.

I am looking for AI consulting and business-facing AI specialist work in Germany, where both halves of that background get used: building the thing, and making the case for it.
